The brief

The judging, leaderboard and big-screen app for the Northern Warrior pairs competition. It has to be bold and competitive, read on a judge's phone, a browser tab and a TV, and sit in the Northern Warrior black-and-gold family.

competitiveliveboldprecise

Success: reads as WL at 16 px, no trophy/podium clichés, works in one colour and reversed, clearly part of Northern Warrior.

Final design

Sprint WL

A W that climbs peak by peak, and a gold L leaning forward beside it like a sprint finish.

  • Every stroke at the same 75° lean: one motion, built on one stroke width
  • The climb says 'moving up the board'; the gold L is the leader
  • Black and gold ties it to Northern Warrior Competitions
  • Small-size cut with a wider W–L gap holds up at 16 px
